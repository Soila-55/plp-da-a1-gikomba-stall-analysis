# Gikomba Market Stall Performance Analysis

Name: Gloria Soila
Cohort:1 data analytics

 Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded her daily sales and stock levels for two weeks. She wants to understand how her business is performing, whether sales are growing, which days perform best, and which products need to be restocked. This analysis uses core Python to examine sales performance, weekly growth, day classifications, weekday versus weekend performance, stock levels, and gross margins. The findings provide practical recommendations to help Wanjiku improve sales and manage her

Top Three Findings

1. Total sales for the 14 days were **KES 48,400**, with Week 2 sales increasing by 19.50% compared with Week 1.

2. Weekend average sales were **KES 4,625**, which was **54.68% higher** than the weekday average of KES 2,990.

3. Tomatoes and Sukuma** need to be reordered because their stock quantities are at or below their reorder levels.
